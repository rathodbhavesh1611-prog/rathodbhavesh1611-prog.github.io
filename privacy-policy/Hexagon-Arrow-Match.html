BR Studio operates the mobile game Hexagon Arrow Match ("Application"). This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you play our game and the choices you have associated with that data.

By downloading, installing, or playing Hexa Away, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

1. Information Collection and Use

We do not collect any personally identifiable information (PII) directly from you. However, the App uses third-party services that may collect information used to identify you, improve gameplay, deliver advertisements, and analyze game performance.

Analytics Data

We use third-party analytics services to understand how players interact with the game. This helps us optimize performance, design better levels, and resolve bugs. The information collected may include device type, operating system version, gameplay sessions, levels completed, and in-game events.

Advertising Data

To keep the App free to play, we display advertisements. Third-party ad networks may collect and use advertising identifiers (such as the Google Advertising ID or Apple IDFA) to serve personalized advertisements based on your interests.

2. Third-Party Service Providers

We integrate the following third-party services in Hexa Away:

  • Google Play Services: Used for game licensing, achievements, leaderboard tracking, and core store services. Google Privacy Policy

  • Firebase Analytics: Used to track gameplay logs and app performance telemetry. Firebase Privacy Policy

3. Children's Privacy

Our App is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and you are aware that your child has provided a third-party service in our App with personal data, please contact us so we can take necessary actions.

4. Data Retention and Security

We do not host or store personal data on our own servers. Any data collected by our third-party partners is stored securely on their respective systems and is subject to their own security policies.

5. Your Rights and Choices

You can manage or opt-out of personalized advertisements through your mobile device settings:

  • Android: Go to Settings > Google > Ads > Opt out of Ads Personalization (or Reset advertising ID).
  • iOS: Go to Settings > Privacy > Advertising > Limit Ad Tracking (or Reset Identifier).
